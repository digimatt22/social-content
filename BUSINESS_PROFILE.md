# MattMadeMe Business Profile

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## Business Snapshot

MattMadeMe is a maker-led business creating original, high-quality 3D printed ducks and whimsical collectibles. The brand sells primarily through Etsy and uses MattMadeMe.com as the owned home for the product showroom, maker story, blog, giveaway, upcoming designs, and community touchpoints.

## Public Positioning

- Core offer: Original 3D printed ducks and creative gifts with personality, charm, and story.
- Brand promise: Fun, whimsical, collectible ducks designed to make people smile.
- Primary sales channel: Etsy shop at `mattmademe.etsy.com`.
- Owned website: `https://mattmademe.com`.
- Materials: PETG for durable, high-quality 3D prints.
- Production/design notes: Ducks are modeled in Nomad Sculpt on iPad and printed on Bambu Lab printers with AMS multicolor systems.
- Differentiator: Original designs created from scratch rather than mass-produced or licensed characters.

## Founder Story

MattMadeMe is run by Matthew Wood, a product engineer specializing in electrical and software design. His background in additive manufacturing, precise engineering, and mass manufacturing influences the quality and design approach behind the ducks.

The business started when Matt designed a 3D printed duck for his wife. That first duck became part of their personal love language and grew into a creative business built around playful, theme-driven duck designs.

## Customer Personas

### Persona 1: Cruise Duck Hunter

Cruise customers buy ducks to hide, gift, trade, or collect before and during sailings. They are likely drawn to designs that feel vacation-ready, ship-specific, funny, easy to pack, and fun to discover in public spaces. This segment matters because cruise ducking is already part of the brand story and website content.

### Persona 2: Gift Buyer

Gift buyers are looking for a small, personality-filled item that matches someone's hobby, job, state, trip, holiday, or inside joke. They may buy only once, but they convert when a duck feels unusually specific to the recipient.

### Persona 3: Collector / Completionist

Collectors appear to be an important repeat-customer segment. They buy across multiple designs, respond to new launches, and may treat the ducks as a growing series rather than one-off novelty gifts. The CSV export shows that repeat customers are only 6.9% of unique customers but account for 30.7% of exported order value, making this group disproportionately valuable.

### Persona 4: Hobby, Profession, Or Identity Buyer

These buyers purchase because a duck maps directly to a role, interest, place, identity, or community. Examples include biker, bowling, astronaut, Florida, room steward, delivery, and other theme-specific ducks.

### Persona 5: Event And Convention Buyer

This segment includes conventions, clubs, associations, and companies ordering custom or bulk ducks as giveaways, attendee gifts, table swag, or community tokens. Recent signals include a custom FLRLCA convention order and a quote for another convention giveaway. This may be a higher-value lane than one-off Etsy orders and should be marketed directly with clear bulk/custom-order messaging.

## New Duck Launch Strategy

New duck launches should be treated as repeat-customer and collector moments, not only new listings.

Recommended launch pattern:

- Tease WIP designs on Instagram and Facebook before listing.
- Collect comments/polls to identify strongest design angles and names.
- Launch to email list and repeat buyers first when possible.
- Add the new duck to MattMadeMe.com with a short story, use case, and Etsy CTA.
- Bundle or cross-link new ducks with related best sellers.
- Track first 7, 30, and 90 days of units sold, conversion rate, favorites, and repeat-customer purchases.
- For seasonal ducks, launch early enough for Etsy search indexing and holiday shipping windows.
