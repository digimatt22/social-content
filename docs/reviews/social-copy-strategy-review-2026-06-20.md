# Social Copy Strategy Review - 2026-06-20

## Strategist Read

The current generated posts are usable but too close to a sales-caption formula:

1. Quick hook.
2. Product description or feature summary.
3. CTA.

That structure is accurate, but it makes the middle paragraph feel like a compressed Etsy listing. The product facts are doing the emotional job, which makes the copy feel salesy even when the CTA is soft.

## Pattern Seen

- Facebook drafts open with a decent question, then explain the duck's accessories and use cases.
- Instagram drafts give the visual a hook, then summarize the product instead of adding personality to the image.
- Pinterest drafts are closer to acceptable because search needs product language, but they still need a save-worthy gift idea or occasion context.

## Direction

Future social copy should choose a story move before product details:

- Tiny scene: where the duck seems to be or what tiny job it has.
- Surprise detail: one odd detail that makes someone pause.
- Playful opinion: a small claim the audience can react to.
- Community prompt: where to hide it, name it, gift it, collect it, or send it next.
- Collector observation: why this tiny theme belongs in a flock.
- Gift moment: who this is for before what it is.
- Maker detail: a real process note when supplied.

Product facts should arrive as proof after the story move, usually limited to 1-2 details for Facebook and Instagram. If the body could be pasted into an Etsy listing with minimal changes, it needs a rewrite.

## Rolled Into Skills

- `social-media-strategist`: now requires a `story_move` and rejects strategies that are only product promotion.
- `social-media-copywriter`: now requires story-first drafting and metadata for the chosen story move.
- `social-post-playbook`: now defines story moves and platform-specific body guidance.
- `social-quality-gate`: now fails `hook -> product description -> CTA` drafts.
- `examples`: now includes a salesy-middle versus social-middle example.
