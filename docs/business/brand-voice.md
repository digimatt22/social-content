# MattMadeMe Brand Voice

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## Voice Pillars

- Whimsical: The brand should feel playful, smiley, and a little punny.
- Maker-led: Matt's engineering and 3D printing craft should show through without making the brand feel cold or technical.
- Original: Emphasize that these are original designs created from scratch, not mass-produced or licensed characters.
- Community-minded: The brand should invite people into the flock through cruise ducking, gifting, collecting, and sharing.
- Warm and personal: The origin story with Matt's wife is part of the emotional center of the business.

## Tone

Use friendly, upbeat language with light humor. Puns are welcome when they support the product, but the copy should still clearly explain what the duck is, who it is for, and why someone would buy it.

## Messaging Themes

- Original 3D printed ducks with personality.
- Small gifts that feel personal and specific.
- Cruise-ready, giftable, and collectible.
- Designed by an engineer, made with care, and built for joy.
- Every duck has a story and a reason to make someone smile.

## Useful Phrases

- Add a duck to your flock.
- Cruise-ready gift.
- Desk mascot.
- Collectible 3D printed duck.
- Made to make someone smile.
- A tiny gift with a lot of personality.

## Avoid

- Overly generic gift-shop language.
- Claims that imply the ducks are rubber ducks.
- Licensed-character language or references that create IP confusion.
- Copy that hides the fact these are 3D printed PETG collectibles.
