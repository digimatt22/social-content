## Strategic Product Read

- Mailman Duck is the flagship proven seller, but marketing should not rely on it alone.
- Postal-adjacent products remain meaningful when Mailman Duck, Mailwoman Duck, and Delivery Duck are viewed together.
- First-responder designs are a major cluster: Firefighter Duck, EMT Duck, Police Duck, and 911 Dispatcher Duck all appear in the top lifetime sellers.
- Bingo, Biker, Declaration, Room Steward, and Casino Cruise show that new or recently active designs can quickly create meaningful demand.
- Launch tracking should compare every new duck against first 30 days, first 90 days, and units per active day, not just lifetime totals.

## New Duck Launch Strategy

New duck launches should be treated as repeat-customer and collector moments, not only new listings.

Recommended launch pattern:

- Tease WIP designs on Instagram and Facebook before listing.
- Collect comments/polls to identify strongest design angles and names.
- Launch to email list and repeat buyers first when possible.
- Add the new duck to MattMadeMe.com with a short story, use case, and Etsy CTA.
- Bundle or cross-link new ducks with related best sellers.
- Track first 7, 30, and 90 days of units sold, conversion rate, favorites, and repeat-customer purchases.
- For seasonal ducks, launch early enough for Etsy search indexing and holiday shipping windows.
