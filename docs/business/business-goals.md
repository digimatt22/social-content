# MattMadeMe Business Goals

Last updated: 2026-06-17

## Purpose

These goals define how marketing tactics should be judged for MattMadeMe. A recommended campaign, content idea, calendar entry, or channel experiment should clearly support at least one of these goals.

## Global Business Goals

### 1. Grow Etsy Sales

Increase Etsy orders and revenue by improving product discovery, conversion, seasonal campaigns, and product-specific promotion.

Success signals:

- More Etsy visits from marketing channels.
- Higher Etsy orders and item revenue.
- Stronger conversion around promoted products.
- Increased sales during seasonal windows and new product launches.

### 2. Increase Repeat Customers

Encourage existing customers and collectors to return for new ducks, themed drops, bundles, seasonal releases, and limited-time promotions.

Success signals:

- Higher repeat customer rate.
- Higher repeat order share.
- More sales from collector-oriented launches.
- More customers buying across multiple product themes.

### 3. Grow Social Media Followers

Expand the audience on active social channels so MattMadeMe has more direct reach for product launches, seasonal promotions, customer stories, and community-building content.

Success signals:

- More Instagram followers.
- More Facebook followers or group/community engagement.
- More comments, shares, saves, and customer photo submissions.
- Increased reach on launch, behind-the-scenes, and community posts.

### 4. Build An Owned Email List

Grow an email list that MattMadeMe controls directly, reducing dependence on social algorithms and marketplace discovery.

Success signals:

- More email subscribers.
- More website visitors joining the list.
- Email campaigns driving Etsy visits and orders.
- Repeat buyers and collectors joining the list for new duck releases.

### 5. Promote New Duck Releases

Create repeatable launch momentum for new duck designs so each release has a clear path from teaser to announcement to follow-up promotion.

Success signals:

- New ducks receive launch content across relevant channels.
- First 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day sales are tracked.
- New releases are introduced to collectors and repeat buyers.
- Strong designs are identified early and folded into future campaigns.

## Tactic Evaluation Rubric

When evaluating a marketing tactic, score it against these criteria:

- Goal alignment: Which global business goal does it support?
- Expected impact: Low, medium, or high.
- Effort required: Low, medium, or high.
- Time sensitivity: Is it tied to a season, launch, event, or trend?
- Reuse potential: Can the idea become a repeatable template or workflow?
- Measurement plan: What metric will show whether it worked?

Prefer tactics with high goal alignment, medium-to-high expected impact, realistic effort, and a clear measurement plan.
