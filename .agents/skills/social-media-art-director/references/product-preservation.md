# Product Preservation Checklist

Before approving generated creative, check:

- The silhouette matches the source product.
- Colors match the source product.
- 3D print layer lines and material texture remain visible where applicable.
- Accessories, clothing, props, facial details, and text elements are unchanged.
- The duck reads as a miniature object in a full-size environment.
- Contact shadows and grounding are believable.
- The output does not add misleading product details.
- The local generated file exists and is registered before approval.
- Reviewer evidence is present for approved generated creative.

Keep the asset in review or reject it when any product identity check fails.
