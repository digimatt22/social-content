# Image Quality Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Destination and format are clear.
- Subject is obvious at thumbnail size.
- Composition has a single primary focal point.
- Lighting supports the mood and product/category.
- Style matches the brand direction.
- Aspect ratio matches the destination.
- No unwanted text, watermark, distorted hands/faces, or irrelevant objects.
- Product/reference-image identity is preserved when required.
- Output is reviewable before production use.

## Common Fixes

- Too generic: add a concrete setting, audience, use case, or brand style.
- Too cluttered: reduce props and background complexity.
- Weak product focus: specify foreground placement, crop, and focal length.
- Wrong destination: revise aspect ratio and safe-zone instructions.
- Risky product preservation: switch to Magnific/Freepik MCP handoff with reference-image roles.

## Review Labels

- `ready_for_generation`: prompt is complete enough to generate.
- `needs_detail`: missing visual details but not blocked.
- `needs_reference`: product or identity preservation requires source imagery.
- `needs_review`: generated output needs human review.
- `rejected`: output is misleading, off-brand, or product-inaccurate.
