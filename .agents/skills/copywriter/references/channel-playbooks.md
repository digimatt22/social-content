# Channel Playbooks

Use the closest playbook when a destination is ambiguous.

## Social Posts

Best for awareness, conversation, product storytelling, launches, and lightweight offers.

Include:

- Hook in the first sentence.
- One clear idea.
- Concrete detail from the source facts.
- CTA matched to the goal.
- Hashtags only when natural and platform-appropriate.

Platform notes:

- Facebook: conversational, community-oriented, works well with questions and short stories.
- Instagram: visual-first, concise caption, sensory/product detail, 3-8 relevant hashtags only when requested.
- LinkedIn: insight, credibility, professional relevance, restrained CTA.
- Pinterest: search-friendly product/use-case phrasing and concise description.

## Newsletter

Best for owned-audience updates, launches, offers, education, and retention.

Include:

- 3-5 subject line options.
- Preview text.
- Section headline.
- Body copy with a clear reason to care.
- Primary CTA.
- Optional secondary link or product note.

Keep paragraphs short. Make the offer or value clear without sounding like a generic sales blast.

## Blog Article

Best for education, search, product stories, guides, and evergreen content.

Include:

- 3 title options.
- Meta description.
- Search intent or reader intent.
- Outline with H2/H3 structure.
- Draft body or section drafts.
- Internal-link suggestions if known.
- CTA that matches the article intent.

Use source facts. Mark any unsupported claims as assumptions or omit them.

## Product Copy

Best for product pages, listings, catalogs, and merchandising.

Include:

- Plain-language product description.
- Who it is for.
- Key benefits or use cases.
- Materials/specs only when provided.
- Care, sizing, compatibility, or shipping only when provided.

Avoid inflated claims and keyword stuffing.

## Ads And Campaign Copy

Best for paid or campaign-specific assets.

Include:

- Primary text.
- Headline options.
- CTA.
- Audience angle.
- Offer proof or source fact.
- Compliance-sensitive claim check.

Prefer direct, specific copy. Avoid manufacturing urgency.
