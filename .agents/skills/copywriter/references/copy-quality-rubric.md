# Copy Quality Rubric

Score copy before returning final output.

## Pass Criteria

- The destination and format are clear.
- The audience is explicit or reasonably inferred.
- The copy uses supplied details or source facts.
- The hook creates a reason to keep reading.
- The CTA matches the goal.
- The voice matches the supplied or inferred brand voice.
- The draft avoids unsupported claims.
- The draft avoids banned terms and known off-brand language.
- The length and structure fit the destination.

For social posts:

- A specific audience angle appears before product details.
- The first line is not just an announcement or product title.
- The CTA asks for one clear platform-native action.
- The draft would still make sense if the product image were removed.

## Common Fixes

- Too generic: add a concrete product, audience, occasion, or benefit detail.
- Too listing-like: choose a social angle and rewrite from the reader's reason to care.
- Too long: remove setup, qualifiers, and repeated claims.
- Weak CTA: make the next action clear and low-friction.
- Off voice: revise sentence rhythm and vocabulary before changing facts.
- Unsupported claim: remove it or mark it as an assumption needing confirmation.

## Social Fail Conditions

Mark `needs_rewrite` if a social draft:

- mostly restates the Etsy title or product description;
- starts with "Check out," "Introducing," or "New in the shop" by default;
- has no audience emotion, story, opinion, question, or use case;
- stacks multiple CTAs;
- invents popularity, reviews, urgency, scarcity, discounts, or events.

## Review Labels

- `ready`: usable after normal human review.
- `needs_light_edit`: direction is right but voice, length, or CTA needs tuning.
- `needs_rewrite`: structural or factual issues make revision more efficient than editing.
- `blocked`: missing required facts, legal/compliance review, or unclear destination.
