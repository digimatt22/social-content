# Copy Review Rules

Generated copy must stay human-reviewed.

Approve or recommend approval only when the draft:

- Uses real product or campaign facts from the repo.
- Has a clear audience, hook, body, and CTA.
- Sounds conversational and specific to MattMadeMe.
- Avoids fake scarcity, unsupported claims, and internal workflow labels.
- Avoids copying planning notes into public-facing copy.
- Fits the selected channel.

Use `needs_rewrite` when a draft is directionally useful but misses voice, specificity, CTA, or source-fact grounding.

Use `rejected` when a draft includes false product claims, misleading urgency, wrong product details, or public-facing text that would require a full restart.
