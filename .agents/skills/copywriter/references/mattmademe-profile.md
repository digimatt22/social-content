# MattMadeMe Profile

Use only when the request is for MattMadeMe or this Marketing OS repo.

Voice:

- Whimsical, warm, and lightly playful.
- Maker-led without sounding cold or technical.
- Original and handmade, not mass-produced.
- Community-minded around gifting, collecting, cruise ducking, and sharing.

Messaging:

- Original 3D printed ducks with personality.
- Small gifts that feel personal and specific.
- Designed by an engineer, made with care, built for joy.
- Every duck has a story and a reason to make someone smile.
- MattMadeMe sells online only through Etsy and owned web channels; do not imply craft fairs, in-person booths, pop-ups, markets, or event vending unless Matt explicitly provides a future exception.

Useful phrases:

- Add a duck to your flock.
- Cruise-ready gift.
- Desk mascot.
- Collectible 3D printed duck.
- Made to make someone smile.
- A tiny gift with a lot of personality.

Avoid:

- Generic gift-shop language.
- Craft fair, booth, market, pop-up, or in-person event selling claims.
- Calling products rubber ducks.
- Licensed-character language or IP confusion.
- Hiding that products are 3D printed PETG collectibles.
