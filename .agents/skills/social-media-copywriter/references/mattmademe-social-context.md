# MattMadeMe Social Context

Use for MattMadeMe social copy only. This is the repo-specific override file; keep general social media guidance in the other reference files so this skill can be reused in other repos by replacing this file.

Preserve accuracy and use placeholders where a product-specific fact is unknown.

## Current Known Positioning

MattMadeMe creates original 3D printed PETG collectible ducks with personality. The copy should feel maker-led, warm, lightly playful, and specific. The products are online-first through Etsy and owned web channels unless Matt provides a future exception.

## Audience Segments

Prioritize Facebook audiences in this order:

1. `Cruise Duckers`: strongest organic community fit; interested in cruise duck hiding, finding ducks onboard ships, cruise duck exchanges, trading, gifting, and sharing.
2. `Gift Buyers`: people buying for coworkers, cruise friends, hobby enthusiasts, birthdays, retirement gifts, room steward gifts, and profession-themed gifts.
3. `Collectors / Flock Builders`: people drawn to state, occupation, cruise, holiday, and lifestyle themed ducks.
4. `Desk Decor Buyers`: people who want desk companions, desk mascots, shelf decor, and workspace personality.
5. `Handmade Shoppers`: Etsy buyers who value original independent-shop items, but usually connect with the character before the manufacturing process.

## Content Pillars

Recommended hierarchy:

- `Cruise And Sharing` - 40%. Cruise duck hiding, room steward gifts, vacation stories, found duck stories, cruise duck exchanges, travel-themed ducks.
- `Duck Personality` - 30%. Meet the duck, character introductions, funny scenarios, "what this duck would do."
- `Flock Building` - 20%. Collections, new releases, state series, occupation series, cruise flock, holiday flock.
- `Maker Process` - 10%. Design sketches, print videos, painting/assembly, behind the scenes.

Strategic emphasis:

- Own `Cruise And Sharing` first because it is the clearest differentiator from generic 3D printed collectibles.
- Elevate `Flock Building` because themed series naturally encourage repeat purchases.
- Use `Duck Personality` to make individual ducks shareable.

## Voice

Use:

- warm, specific, and lightly playful;
- short hooks with a little personality;
- concrete product details translated into a human reason to care;
- invitation over hype;
- cruise duck community language when relevant;
- "flock," "desk mascot," "tiny sidekick," "collectible 3D printed duck," when natural.

Core phrases:

- Desk Mascot
- Tiny Sidekick
- Ready for the Assignment
- Newest Member of the Flock
- Small Duck. Big Personality.

Additional phrases:

- Built for Adventure
- Quack With Purpose
- Collectible With Character
- Waddle Into Your Collection
- Professional Troublemaker
- Pocket-Sized Personality
- Bringing Chaos and Charm
- Ready to Join Your Crew

Avoid:

- generic gift-shop language;
- aggressive sales CTAs such as "Buy now!" or fake urgency;
- repeating Etsy titles as the hook;
- "Check out," "Introducing," and "New in the shop" as default openings;
- calling products rubber ducks;
- implying craft fairs, booths, markets, pop-ups, or in-person event selling;
- licensed-character wording or IP confusion;
- fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake customer quotes, or unsupported popularity claims.

Avoid "Limited time!" style urgency unless Matt supplies a real time-bound offer.

## CTAs

Primary Etsy CTA:

- "Find your favorite duck in our Etsy shop."

Secondary variations:

- "Add a new duck to your flock."
- "See the full flock on Etsy."
- "Which duck belongs on your desk?"
- "Grab yours before it waddles away."
- "Start your flock today."

CTA guidance:

- Prefer collectible, community-driven, personality-based CTAs.
- Avoid aggressive sales language.
- For engagement posts, ask a question before asking for a shop click.
- For shop-click posts, use one Etsy CTA only.
