# Social Context

Use this as the reusable strategy layer for social posts.

## Social Goals

Map each request to one primary goal:

- `engagement`: comments, replies, saves, shares, conversation.
- `follower_growth`: make the brand/person worth following.
- `traffic`: drive shop, profile, product, or site clicks.
- `conversion`: support purchase intent without sounding like a listing.
- `trust`: show process, judgment, proof, or care.
- `community`: invite participation, naming, opinions, stories, or collecting.

## Content Types

- `educational`: teaches one useful thing; CTA asks what to add, save, or share.
- `storytelling`: starts with a moment; CTA invites similar stories or reactions.
- `promotional`: sells one thing through a human reason to care.
- `engagement`: asks a specific question people can answer quickly.
- `behind_the_scenes`: reveals process, decision, mistake, material, or craft.
- `personality`: gives the subject a memorable point of view.

Promotional posts should usually be a minority of a social feed. If the user asks for follower-building or engagement, avoid making the product link the emotional center of the post.

## Angle Before Copy

Select one primary angle:

- `giftable`: who would give it and why.
- `collectible`: why someone would want it in a set or collection.
- `personality`: what character, mood, or story the item suggests.
- `maker_process`: what making/designing it reveals.
- `occasion`: holiday, trip, desk refresh, thank-you, birthday, cruise, teacher gift.
- `community_prompt`: question, choice, naming prompt, this-or-that, collector prompt.
- `shop_action`: direct but still human reason to click.

If no angle is supplied, infer one from the product facts and goal. State the inference.

## CTA Types

- Comment CTA: "Which one should join the flock next?"
- Share CTA: "Send this to the friend whose desk needs a tiny sidekick."
- Save CTA: "Save this for your next gift idea list."
- Shop CTA: use the brand's supplied shop wording; for MattMadeMe, prefer "Find your favorite duck in our Etsy shop."
- Follow CTA: "Follow for more tiny ducks with very specific jobs."
- Community CTA: "What would you name this one?"

Use one primary CTA. Do not stack several asks.
