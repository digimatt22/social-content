# Social Hooks

Generate 5-7 hooks before drafting. Use only patterns that can be supported by the supplied facts.

## Hook Patterns

### Personality Hook

Gives the subject a memorable tiny story.

- "This duck looks like it has a very specific job."
- "This little desk mascot came dressed for the assignment."

Use for follower-building, comments, product personality, and visual posts.

### Gift Moment Hook

Connects the product to a giver and recipient.

- "For the friend whose desk needs a tiny sidekick."
- "A small gift for the person who notices the fun details."

Use for gift buyers and shop-click goals.

### Community Question Hook

Starts with an easy-to-answer prompt.

- "Which duck belongs in your flock next?"
- "What would you name this one?"

Use for engagement and Facebook.

### Behind-The-Scenes Hook

Starts from a real making or design detail.

- "This one started as [MATT_TO_CONFIRM: real design detail]."
- "The tiny detail that makes this duck work: [MATT_TO_CONFIRM: detail]."

Use only with sourced process facts.

### Occasion Hook

Connects the item to a concrete use case.

- "Cruise duckers, this one is ready to travel."
- "Desk refresh, but make it tiny and weirdly charming."

Use for seasonal, cruise, office, teacher, or birthday content.

### Contrast Hook

Sets up a small surprise without overclaiming.

- "Not every gift needs to be practical."
- "Some desk decor blends in. This duck does not."

Use when the body can pay off the contrast.

### List Hook

Promises quick value or choice.

- "Three reasons this duck makes a better desk mascot than a sticky note."
- "A tiny gift idea for the person who already has everything."

Use sparingly for social posts and only when the list is real.

## Hook Selection

Pick the hook that:

- makes the audience feel addressed;
- creates a question the body answers;
- is specific enough that it could not apply to every product;
- fits the first-line truncation of the platform;
- does not require unsupported facts.

Reject hooks that merely announce the product name, repeat the Etsy title, or start like a catalog listing.
