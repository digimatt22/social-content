# Social Copy Examples

## Weak To Strong

Weak:

```text
Check out our Mailman Duck, a 3D printed collectible available on Etsy.
```

Why it fails:

- starts like an ad;
- repeats product category;
- gives no reason to comment, follow, or care.

Stronger:

```text
This little mail carrier looks like he has one very important delivery left.

Probably snacks.

The Mailman Duck is a 3D printed collectible made for desks, shelves, and tiny flock collections.

Who would you send this one to?
```

## Salesy Middle To Social Middle

Salesy:

```text
Biker Duck brings motorcycle attitude in collectible 3D printed form, with a helmet, vest, and grey accents. It fits rider gifts, cruise duck hiding, or a desk that needs an open-road mood.
```

Why it fails:

- summarizes features in listing order;
- tries to serve every use case at once;
- gives the audience nothing to react to.

Stronger:

```text
This one looks like it parked outside for exactly two minutes and already has a story.

Tiny helmet, tiny vest, big "I know a shortcut" energy.

Who in your flock would claim the biker duck first?
```

## Hook And CTA Good, Middle Weak

Weak:

```text
Every flock needs one duck that looks like it has stories from the open road.

Biker Duck feels like the shelf-side rebel of the group, with a black helmet, black vest, grey accents, and the kind of collectible 3D printed attitude that makes a desk setup feel a little more fun.

Which duck in your flock would park next to this one?
```

Why it fails:

- the hook and CTA are social, but the middle turns back into product-description copy;
- the feature stack interrupts the personality bit;
- "collectible 3D printed attitude" sounds like caption filler instead of something a person would say.

Stronger:

```text
Every flock needs one duck that looks like it has stories from the open road.

This one feels like it would lean against the edge of the shelf, refuse to explain where it has been, and somehow convince three other ducks to follow it anyway.

The black helmet and vest just make the attitude official.

Which duck in your flock would park next to this one?
```

## Forced Fact To Cleaner Flow

Forced:

```text
This one looks like it owns a tiny garage.

The black helmet, black vest, grey accents, PETG material, and motorcycle theme make it a fun 3D printed collectible.

Who would park this one on their desk?
```

Why it fails:

- the second line stops the bit to prove the product exists;
- most details are obvious or generic;
- the voice becomes robotic.

Stronger:

```text
This one looks like it owns a tiny garage.

Probably has a very specific parking spot on the shelf, too.

Who would park this one on their desk?
```

Use product facts only when they make the post sharper, funnier, more credible, or more useful.

## Proof-Led Product Story

Weak:

```text
Mailman Duck is a customizable 3D printed collectible with a postal-style cap and letter. It makes a great gift for mail carriers, postal workers, cruise duck hiding, or desk decor.
```

Why it fails:

- describes the listing instead of the reason people care;
- treats postal workers, coworkers, collectors, and cruise duckers as interchangeable;
- misses the appreciation story.

Stronger when demand proof is supplied:

```text
Mail Duck did not take off because people needed another tiny thing for a shelf.

It took off because people wanted a small, funny way to say thank you to the person who keeps showing up.

[MATT_TO_CONFIRM: order count or recent demand signal] orders have gone to people gifting mail carriers, postal coworkers, and the route regulars who somehow know every porch, dog, and mailbox on the block.

The customizable hat makes it personal. The real story is the appreciation.
```

Stronger when demand proof is not supplied:

```text
There is a very specific kind of person who deserves a tiny duck with a mailbag.

The coworker who always covers the route.
The carrier who knows every name on the street.
The friend whose desk already has three "just one more" collectibles.

Mail Duck is customizable, but the better part is who it lets you think of.
```

## Engagement Variant

```text
Which duck belongs in the flock next?

I keep coming back to the ones with tiny jobs, tiny outfits, and a little too much personality for their size.

What theme should I make next?
```

## Follower-Building Variant

```text
Some ducks are just ducks.

This one looks like it has a shift at 8, a snack break at 10, and very strong opinions about desk organization.

Follow along for more tiny 3D printed ducks with oddly specific jobs.
```

## Shop-Click Variant

```text
For the friend whose desk needs a tiny sidekick.

This 3D printed duck is made to be a small, personality-packed gift for shelves, offices, and collections.

Find your favorite duck in our Etsy shop.
```
